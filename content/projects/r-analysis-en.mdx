# R Analysis — Shopping Cart Button Impact Analysis

## Screenshot
![R Analysis Web](/og/labs/r-analysis/web.png)

**Tags**: R, VanillaJS

This Lab uses R to build an analysis model exploring how shopping cart button design affects purchase conversion. VanillaJS powers the interactive visualization layer for decision support and iteration.

## Focus
- Model assumptions and variable selection
- Experiment design and A/B comparison
- Visualization and insight presentation

## Study 1: cart button colour and purchasing
Using data across countries and devices, compares two cart-button colour groups to see whether colour, culture or device changes purchase behaviour, visualised with box plots, time-series lines and density curves.

## Study 2: gut microbiome in CKD patients vs. healthy controls
Clusters 16S rRNA sequencing data into an OTU table (one OTU per bacterium), compares group distance with PCA and PCoA, checks correlations between genera, then compares the relative abundance of selected genera across the two groups.
