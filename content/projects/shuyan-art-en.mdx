## Overview
This Lab presents a design freelance brand through a refined portfolio experience, clear service positioning, and a lightweight path for potential clients to understand the work and start a conversation.

## Content structure
- Home: the designer's positioning and selected work (brand website design, digital art, minimalism, conceptual photography)
- Services: UI/UX, brand planning and visual design, with the collaboration flow from brief to delivery
- Pricing reference, including discounted plans for independent studios and students
- Conversion: a free 15–20 minute design consultation and a persistent "Let's talk" button in the nav

## Tech
- Next.js with Velite managing works and posts, so adding a piece is one new content file
- Generous whitespace, light type and a single tone keep the focus on the work
