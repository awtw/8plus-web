# Kidney Learning — Pediatric CKD Clinic

![Kidney Learning dashboard](/og/labs/ckd2026/web.png)

**Tags**: Vue 3, TypeScript, Vite, Supabase, Vitest, Vercel

## Why
Pediatric CKD education often relies on paper handouts and verbal explanation. Clinic time is short, so it is hard to confirm whether learners and families actually learned. The pediatric immunology and nephrology teaching clinic at Taipei Veterans General Hospital wanted a tool, usable in the clinic or after class, that makes learning outcomes visible and comparable. (The UI is in Traditional Chinese.)

The first version was a front-end-only site with a five-question pre/post test; in late 2026 it grew into a full clinical teaching platform.

## Learning flow
1. **Pre-test**: 15 questions, three in each of five areas (kidney function, CKD staging, common causes, complications, treatment choices).
2. **Teaching clinic**: enter patient data, practise staging CKD, and get a clinic report.
3. **Lab reading**: 100 synthetic patients. Read growth, kidney and urine, blood pressure and electrolytes, anaemia and bone-mineral values, make 9 judgements, then see the explanation; retry mistakes, download backups and generate reports.
4. **Clinical reasoning**: interview a simulated child and mother, recording hypotheses, evidence and revisions at each stage; core labs unlock only after the first reasoning snapshot, practising re-ranking hypotheses on new data.
5. **Post-test**: retest to see progress.

## Teachers and accounts
- Learners register cloud accounts with an **invite code** and submit tests and reasoning to their class; teacher rights are assigned by an admin.
- The teacher console searches learners, shows raw answer snapshots and scores on a **six-dimension rubric**; every score must cite the learner's own words, each publish is kept as a new version, and a separate safety review sits outside the average.
- Legacy local records are never auto-assigned to new accounts, avoiding misattribution.
- A demo page uses a showcase account and fictional classmates to present tests, clinic reports and teacher reports without exposing real learners (images below are simulated).

## Benchmarks and resources
- Legacy five-question (10-point) scores: 166 pre-tests, mean 6.14 / median 6; 162 post-tests, mean 7.98 / median 8. Shown as distributions and percentile tables, noting that pre/post are unpaired and the unit is attempts, not learners.
- Resources are grouped by the five areas, linking PubMed papers and guidelines (Schwartz equation, CKiD U25, KDIGO 2024 and more) with source, year and check date.

## Tech
- Front end: Vue 3 + TypeScript + Vite, hash routing, lazily loaded pages
- Back end: Supabase (email accounts, tables and RPCs for classes, invite codes, clinic reports, attempts and score versions)
- Charts: hand-built SVG radar and distribution components
- Testing: Vitest, vue-tsc type checking
- Data prep: Python turns historical spreadsheets into anonymous statistics (distribution, mean, median only)
- Hosting: Vercel; the footer shows the build version and changelog
- Models: clinical reasoning uses a **rule-based script** so replies stay consistent with the case; no generative AI yet

## Limitations
- Simulated patients and labs are synthetic teaching data, not real patients or prevalence, and not for medical use.
- Practice progress lives in the browser and doesn't sync across devices; submit to the cloud account or download a backup to keep it.
- Unrecognised questions to the rule-based patient get a neutral reply.
