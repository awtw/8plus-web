# B18 Brand Website — basement18 studio

![basement18 studio home](/og/labs/b18/web.png)

**Tags**: Next.js, Vercel, Instagram, UI/UX Design

## Brand and world
B18 is basement level 18 on an elevator panel: B for basement, 18 for the eighteen levels of hell in Chinese folk belief, and also a number tied to Guanyin. The label's line is roughly "only hell knows how good heaven is", deconstructing traditional religious symbols with contemporary fashion language to explore human nature.

The logo borrows an elevator floor marker: three blurred, undefinable figures that also spell "B18", standing for the many sides of human nature.

## Site structure
- **Home**: the B18 STUDIO wordmark with a scrolling tagline, then collections and an Instagram wall.
- **Series**: two lines
  - **B18 GUANX3**: concrete, neon, industrial silhouettes
  - **B18 Temple Office (神明事務所)**: ritual textures, matte blacks, sacred geometry
- **Lookbook**: a wall of styled shots, sharing imagery with Instagram.
- **About**: brand story, logo concept and designer DK Marc's point of view.
- **Contact**: social channels, partner stockists and clothing rental.

## Design and experience
- Dark basement tones, bold condensed headings and red accents make the site feel like stepping down a floor.
- Images keep their original ratios with no extra decoration, letting garments and graphics speak.
- Every section has an exit to Instagram: the site explains the brand, Instagram carries daily updates.
- Mobile first: the first screen is the tagline and two entry points (Explore Series / About the Brand).

![Mobile home](/og/labs/b18/mobile.png)

## Tech
- Next.js, hosted on Vercel
- Collections use dynamic routes (`/series/[slug]`)
- Instagram routing: the gallery and follow buttons link to the official account
